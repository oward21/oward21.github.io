Midnight Room

The Midnight Room at WonderStay Bed & Breakfast offers a modern and relaxing atmosphere with a sophisticated nighttime theme. Dark colors, gray accents, and silver decorations create a stylish space for guests to unwind. The comfortable furnishings and soft lighting make this room a welcoming retreat after a busy day of traveling or exploring.

Room Amenities

Comfortable Bedding
A comfortable bed with dark bedding and soft pillows.
Complimentary Wi-Fi
Internet access for browsing, studying, or staying connected.
Private Bathroom
A private bathroom with fresh towels and basic toiletries.
Storage Space
A closet or storage area for clothing and personal belongings.
Bedside Lighting
Soft reading lamps that provide comfortable lighting in the evening.
Breakfast
A morning breakfast included with the overnight stay.

Room Rates

The Midnight Room starts at $115 per night for up to two guests. The rate includes an overnight stay and breakfast. Prices may vary during holidays and peak travel seasons. Taxes and additional fees may apply.

Special Features

The Midnight Room features a modern color scheme of midnight blue, charcoal gray, and silver. Its dark accents, clean lines, and soft lighting create a peaceful atmosphere with a touch of elegance. This room is ideal for guests who prefer a contemporary style and a quiet place to relax.

Occupancy Information

The Midnight Room accommodates up to two guests, making it suitable for couples or individuals. Guests who need additional accommodations should contact WonderStay Bed & Breakfast before making a reservation.
