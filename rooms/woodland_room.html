Woodland Room

Welcome to the Woodland Room at WonderStay Bed & Breakfast. This room is inspired by the peaceful beauty of nature and provides guests with a cozy place to relax and enjoy their stay. Its woodland theme creates a welcoming atmosphere for visitors who appreciate natural colors and rustic decorations.

A Peaceful Atmosphere

The Woodland Room is designed to give guests a comfortable place to unwind after a busy day. Whether you have spent the day traveling, visiting local attractions, or spending time with friends and family, this room offers a welcoming place to rest and recharge.

Room Amenities

  • Comfortable bed with soft, green bedding
  • Wooden furniture and nature-inspired decorations
  • Bedside tables and reading lamps
  • Closet or storage space for personal belongings
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi
  • Private bathroom with fresh towels and toiletries
  • Daily housekeeping service

Room Rates

The Woodland Room starts at $110 per night for up to two guests. The rate includes a comfortable overnight stay and breakfast. Prices may vary during holidays and peak travel seasons. Please contact WonderStay Bed & Breakfast to confirm availability and current rates. Taxes and additional fees may apply.

Special Features

The Woodland Room stands out with its forest-inspired design, green color palette, and natural wooden accents. Soft lighting and comfortable furnishings help create a relaxing retreat. The room is designed to give guests a cozy place to unwind after a day of traveling or exploring.

Occupancy Information

The Woodland Room accommodates up to two guests and is suitable for couples or individuals. Guests who need additional accommodations should contact WonderStay Bed & Breakfast before booking.
