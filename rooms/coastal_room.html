Coastal Room

The Coastal Room offers a bright and refreshing atmosphere inspired by the beauty of the seaside. Ocean-blue accents, crisp white bedding, and light colored furnishings create a peaceful environment where guests can relax and unwind. This room is ideal for couples or travelers looking for a comfortable getaway.

Room Amenities

The Coastal Room includes comfortable bedding, coastal decorations, complimentary Wi-Fi, and a private bathroom with fresh towels and toiletries.

Room Rates

The Coastal Room starts at $110 per night for up to two guests. The rate includes an overnight stay and breakfast. Prices may vary during holidays and peak travel seasons. Please contact WonderStay Bed & Breakfast to confirm availability and current rates. Taxes and additional fees may apply.

Special Features

The Coastal Room features a seaside-inspired color palette with ocean-blue accents, white bedding, and light wooden decorations. Its airy design and soft lighting create a relaxing atmosphere. Comfortable furnishings provide a welcoming place to rest after a day of traveling or exploring local attractions.

Occupancy Information

The Coastal Room accommodates up to two guests, making it suitable for couples or individuals. Guests who require additional accommodations should contact WonderStay Bed & Breakfast before booking.

Guest Check-In Process

  1. Contact WonderStay to reserve the Coastal Breeze Room.
  2. Arrive at the bed and breakfast on your scheduled check-in date.
  3. Receive your room information and keys from the host.
  4. Settle into your room and enjoy your coastal-inspired getaway.
